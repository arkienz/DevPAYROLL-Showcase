# Easy Payroll marketing set: captions

Images are in `posters-en/` (English) and `posters-tl/` (Taglish); the square slides are in `slides/`. Post size 1080x1350 (4:5, works on Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn). `00-banner-1600x900.png` is the page cover. All screens are real captures of the app running on sample data (Acme Foods Inc., SMP-001 to SMP-006), so the figures are not official rates. Draft government rates used for the sample payslips are unsigned and unverified: do not post an image that shows a contribution amount as if it were an official figure.


## Image order and captions

### 00 Hook (`00-hook.png`)
EN: Your payroll software adds premiums. The law multiplies them. Night overtime at 150 an hour is 206.25 when multiplied (150 x 1.25 x 1.10), not 202.50 when added. That is 3.75 short on every night overtime hour, for every employee. Easy Payroll multiplies them for you. Ask for a free walkthrough on your own cutoff. #PayrollPH #BPOPhilippines #HRPhilippines
TL: Dinadagdag ba ng payroll mo ang premiums? Ang batas, minu-multiply. Ang night overtime sa 150 kada oras ay 206.25 kapag minultiply (150 x 1.25 x 1.10), hindi 202.50. Kulang ng 3.75 bawat oras, bawat empleyado. Ang Easy Payroll ang magma-multiply para sa iyo. Humingi ng libreng walkthrough sa sarili mong cutoff. #PayrollPH #BPOPhilippines #HRPhilippines

### 01 Premiums (`01-premiums.png`)
EN: Every premium is on the payslip: hours, rate, amount. Overtime at 1.25 times the hourly rate, night differential on the night hours, and the premiums stack on rest days and holidays instead of just adding up.
TL: Kita sa payslip ang bawat premium: oras, rate, halaga. Overtime na 1.25 beses ng hourly rate, night differential sa oras ng gabi, at nagse-stack ang premiums sa rest day at holiday.

### 02 Net pay never below zero (`02-no-negative-pay.png`)
EN: Short cutoff and a loan due? Not a negative payslip. Contributions come first, then loans. The loan is cut to what fits, the rest stays on the balance, and HR sees the flagged line before you finalize.
TL: Maikli ang cutoff at may hulog sa loan? Hindi magne-negative ang payslip. Una ang contributions, kasunod ang loans. Binabawasan ang loan ayon sa kasya, ang natira ay nananatili sa balanse, at naka-flag para sa HR bago i-finalize.

### 03 Fixed salary (`03-fixed-salary.png`)
EN: Why is my salary lower this cutoff? Not with fixed amount pay. A short cutoff still pays the full period amount, and only absences, unpaid leave and lateness are deducted. Choose it once in Company settings.
TL: Bakit mas mababa ang sweldo ko ngayong cutoff? Hindi kapag fixed amount ang bayad. Kahit maikli ang cutoff, buo ang period pay at ang absent, unpaid leave at late lang ang ibinabawas. Isang beses lang piliin sa Company settings.

### 04 Contribution timing (`04-contribution-timing.png`)
EN: You choose when SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG are deducted: last cutoff, first cutoff, split evenly or a specific cutoff, set per pay group. The month always adds up exactly once, and a mid-month hire still catches up.
TL: Ikaw ang pipili kailan kukunin ang SSS, PhilHealth at Pag-IBIG: last cutoff, first cutoff, hati o piling cutoff, per pay group. Isang beses lang buo ang buwan, at may catch-up ang mid-month hire.

### 05 Rate packs (`05-rate-packs.png`)
EN: Government rates change. Your payroll should not need a new install. Rates are versioned tables that arrive as a digitally signed rate pack: you review each table, then apply. Unsigned or altered packs are refused.
TL: Nagbabago ang government rates. Hindi dapat kailangan ng reinstall. Versioned tables ang rates na dumarating bilang signed rate pack: ire-review mo ang bawat table bago i-apply. Tinatanggihan ang unsigned o binago.

### 06 Shifts (`06-shifts.png`)
EN: Day, mid, night and rotating shifts. A 9 PM to 6 AM shift belongs to the day it started. Rest days per person, bulk assign, rotating patterns of 2 to 8 weeks, and a warning when two assignments overlap.
TL: Day, mid, night at rotating shifts. Ang 9 PM hanggang 6 AM na shift ay para sa araw na nagsimula ito. May rest days kada tao, bulk assign, rotating na 2 hanggang 8 linggo, at babala kung nag-o-overlap.

### 07 Self-service (`07-self-service.png`)
EN: Your staff help themselves. Their own sign-in with an employee number, clock in (the time is stamped by the server, not the phone or PC), see the week's schedule, announcements, leave balance and their own payslips.
TL: Ang staff mo, sila na ang bahala. Sariling sign-in gamit ang employee no., mag-clock in (server ang nagtatakda ng oras, hindi ang phone o PC), tingnan ang schedule ng linggo, announcements, leave balance at sariling payslip.

### 08 My Schedule (`08-my-schedule.png`)
EN: Every day shows how it went: on time, late, absent, rest day, holiday or leave, on one calendar. If a time log looks wrong, the employee asks HR to fix it, with a reason.
TL: Bawat araw, kita kung ano ang nangyari: on time, late, absent, rest day, holiday o leave sa iisang calendar. Kung mali ang time log, hihingi ang empleyado ng ayos kay HR, may kasamang dahilan.

### 09 Dashboard (`09-dashboard.png`)
EN: See what needs you before payday. Today's figures first, then a numbered Needs you list (time logs to fix, requests, the run) and one button that names the next step: Create, Compute or Review. Each section follows the user's permissions.

### 10 Sign-in screen (`10-sign-in.png`)
EN: A sign-in screen that knows the hour. Sun through the blinds by day, a desk lamp at night, with your company's mark in the scene. Staff sign in with email, employees with an employee number.
TL: Sign-in na alam ang oras. Araw sa bintana sa umaga, lampara sa gabi, kasama ang mark ng kumpanya mo. Email para sa staff, employee number para sa empleyado.

### Claims for the accounting slide (rerun before posting)
- Say Easy Aiccounting is a separate product, licensed separately. Do not call it BIR-registered or accredited books; its reports say "Not a BIR-registered book of accounts."
- The link sends totals of finalized runs only. Say it is read-only and key-protected; do not say it files anything with SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG or BIR (it records and prints only).
- Say "drafts for an accountant to review and post", never "automatic bookkeeping" or "no accountant needed".

## Honest-claims check (rerun before posting)
- Say "most payroll tools", never "no other system".
- Do not say BIR-accredited, SSS-approved or official forms. The government files are data exports.
- Do not say rates are pre-loaded. Rate tables start empty and come from a signed pack the owner publishes.
- The client is a Windows desktop app today. Do not advertise Android or iOS.
- "Night OT 206.25 vs 202.50" is the Labor Code night overtime compounding (125% then 10% of that); keep the math visible so a reader can check it.
- The sample payslips show up to 4 decimals because the app never rounds amounts. If that looks odd in a post, say so in the caption rather than editing the picture.
